    Map interpretation and data sources
    Uncolored areas are NOT verified dead zones. Tower databases can be incomplete. Circular coverage overlays are deliberately simplistic: they do not model terrain, sectors, frequency, transmit power, or received signal strength. For low-RF site selection, validate using band-specific SDR measurements.
